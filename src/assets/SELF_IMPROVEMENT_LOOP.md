## 3. The 4 Geological Upgrade Operators (Recommendations to Apply)

When the self-improvement pass is triggered on any module scoring `< 85/100`, the `geometry-specialist` must apply these four mathematical operators:

### Operator A: 360° Perimeter Strata & Corner Chamfering (Fixes `BoxCling% > 28%` and `360° < 15/20`)
- **Problem**: Modules built from `BoxGeometry` only displaced `if (v.z > 0)`, leaving the left (`-X`), right (`+X`), and back (`-Z`) faces as dead-flat planes (`0%` lateral silhouette variance).
- **Fix**:
  1. Compute horizontal angle $\theta = \text{atan2}(z, x)$ and radial corner factor $c = \max(|x|/W, |z|/D)$.
  2. Bevel the 4 vertical box corners inward by $15\%–25\%$ modulated by stepped columnar block indices so the top-down footprint is an irregular fractured polygon rather than a rectangle.
  3. Apply quantized horizontal sedimentary ledges ($H_{\text{strata}} = 2.4\text{m}$) along the outward radial normal $(\text{sign}(x), 0, \text{sign}(z))$ on **all 4 vertical faces**, allowing any module to be rotated $90^\circ / 180^\circ / 270^\circ$ for kitbashing.

### Operator B: Corbelled Under-Taper (Fixes `FlatBtm% > 45%` and Worm's-Eye Ceiling Artifacts)
- **Problem**: When viewed from the canyon floor (`Photo 2` camera preset), flat bottom faces ($y = -H/2, n_y = -1$) read as artificial floating concrete slabs.
- **Fix**:
  1. In the bottom $18\%$ of height ($u_y = \text{clamp}((y - y_{\min}) / (0.18 H), 0, 1)$), apply an inward corbelled taper or stepped undercut:
     $$\Delta z_{\text{under}} = -(1 - u_y)^2 \cdot 0.35 D, \quad \Delta y_{\text{bottom}} = \text{corbelStep}(x, z) \cdot 0.12 H$$
  2. This breaks up the flat $-Y$ normal hemisphere and tucks the bottom of every upper cliff module smoothly into the underlying wall.

### Operator C: Smooth Exponential Carving instead of Hard Clamping (Fixes Degenerate Triangles in `CLIFF-001` & `CLIFF-005`)
- **Problem**: Setting `if (y < threshold) z = const;` collapses multiple grid rows onto the exact same 3D line, creating $>10\%$ zero-area degenerate triangles and sharp normal discontinuities.
- **Fix**:
  1. Always use smooth $C^1$ blending (`THREE.MathUtils.lerp` with `smoothstep` weights) and preserve a minimum longitudinal gradient ($\partial z / \partial y \neq 0$) across all grid segments.

### Operator D: Canonical World-Space Strata & Parametric Seed Signature
- **Problem**: Adjacent cliff modules use slightly different bedding intervals (`1.5m`, `1.8m`, `2.5m`, `2.8m`), causing horizontal rock ledges to misalign at module boundaries.
- **Fix**:
  1. Standardize primary bedding interval to `CANONICAL_STRATA_INTERVAL = 2.4` meters with sub-beds at `0.8` meters.
  2. Support optional `{ seed = 0, strataOffset = 0, wear = 1.0 }` parameters in every `createCliff*` factory function so multiple instances of the same BOM asset produce organic variations while locking to the same horizontal strata grid.

### 5.2 Eight Honest Quantitative Score Checks (100 Points Total — Zero Hardcoded Points)

Every point in `scripts/audit-visual-match.js` is computed directly from `gl.readPixels` buffers (calibrated so an unblended kitbash assembly with plaid shader banding and poor framing scores `~44–50/100`):

1. **Sky Headroom & Framing Proportion (`15 pts`)**:
   - Measures `skyHeadroomRatio` and `topTouchRatio` in `classic` and `panorama` pure-cliff silhouettes.
   - Deducts up to 15 pts if skyline columns clip the top 4% of the viewport (`topTouchRatio > 2%`) or if sky headroom falls below `18%` in Classic (`25%` in Panorama).
2. **Skyline Mesa Continuity & Zero Protruding Slabs (`15 pts`)**:
   - Measures `rimRangeRatio = (maxRimY - minRimY) / H`, `stepJumps` (`|dy| > 7px`), and `avgDy` across the central mesa (`0.20W..0.80W`).
   - Penalizes backing slabs (`CentralAlcoveSealingBackdrop`) or flanking buttress tops protruding above the horizontal plateau tabletop ($Y = 14.35$).
3. **Multi-Angle Zero Sky-Leak Guarantee (`10 pts`)**:
   - Sums internal sky-leak pixels below the skyline contour across `classic`, `leftOblique`, and `rightOblique` with `hideFoliage: true` (so foreground trees cannot hide gaps in the lower cliff).
4. **Anti-Plaid Shader & Anti-Waffle Clay Relief Entropy (`15 pts`)**:
   - Computes row-averaged horizontal stripe energy (`rowStripeEnergy`), column-averaged vertical stripe energy (`colStripeEnergy`), periodic row zero-crossings (`rowOscillationRate`) in Beauty pass, and `clayWaffleOscillations` in the Clay pass.
   - Penalizes regular `sin(x) * sin(y)` tartan plaid grids in `tepui.frag` and quilted waffle vertex attributes in cliff generators.
5. **Module Coalescence & Anti-Floating-Ledge Inspection (`15 pts`)**:
   - Scans front and 45° oblique Clay passes (`hideFoliage: true`) for isolated bright-top/black-underside islands (`floatingShelfAnomalies` — "floating hotdog shelves" and hovering module bottoms) and sharp horizontal luminance jumps (`verticalSeamJumps` — vertical kitbash seams).
6. **Canopy-to-Cliff Scale & Plunge Pool Visibility (`10 pts`)**:
   - Measures screen-space green foliage fraction (`canopyCoverage`, target `18%–32%`) and water visibility in the central plunge basin (`plungeWaterRatio >= 8%`, `plungeOccludedByTrees < 35%`).
   - Penalizes oversized foreground tree polygons that block the lower cliff and plunge pool.
7. **Cataract Top-Rim Emergence & 1:20 Slenderness (`10 pts`)**:
   - Measures `emergenceGapPx = |notchRimY - waterTopY|` (target `<= 14px`), `slendernessRatio = height / medianWidth` (target `14:1 to 24:1`), and vertical row continuity (`>= 90%`).
8. **Zonal Lithological & Alcove Shadow Contrast (`10 pts`)**:
   - Samples West Amphitheater warmth (`R - B >= 18`), West-vs-East tonal separation (`>= 18`), and Central Wet Alcove shadow darkness (`alcoveLuma / westLuma <= 0.75`).

   - Removes axis-aligned periodic `sin(vWorldPos.y * freq)` and `sin(vWorldPos.x * freq)` tartan/plaid grid stripes.

## 7. Dynamic Audit Self-Evolution ("Ponder & Modify On The Go") & Mandatory 3-Pass Rule

### 7.1 Right & Duty to Ponder and Modify `audit-visual-match.js` On The Go
The Re-Sculpt phase is **explicitly authorized and required to ponder and modify `scripts/audit-visual-match.js` on the go**:
- After rendering `qa-visual-comparison.png`, `qa-fullscene-clay.png`, and `qa-fullscene-silhouette.png`, the agent must visually compare every panel against `salto-angel-photos/`.
- Ask: *"Does the current numeric score in `audit-visual-match.js` match a critical human visual rating, or are there visible flaws (floating blocks, repetitive stripes, bad proportions, unnatural shapes) that the script missed?"*
- Whenever a visual flaw is visible in the PNGs but not penalized by `audit-visual-match.js`, the agent **must first update `scripts/audit-visual-match.js`** to detect and penalize that flaw before running the parallel re-sculpt fixes.
